# International premium and mid-range laptop/commuter backpacks sold online in Türkiye (snapshot 18 Oct 2026)

Method caveats (read first):
- WebFetch could not reach any .tr retailer or thule.com or osprey.com host (DNS errors: trendyol, hepsiburada, amazon.com.tr, akakce, cimri, vatanbilgisayar, samsonite.com.tr, decathlon.com.tr). All Turkish prices below therefore come from search-result summaries of listing pages, not from pages I opened. Most snapshots are undated; treat each as "seen in search around 18 Oct 2026, date of underlying listing unknown" unless a date is given.
- I did not reach 10-14 fully specified candidates. About 8 models have some Turkish price evidence, and only Thule Subterra 2 and Samsonite Securipak 2.0 have usable spec data. Gaps are flagged explicitly.
- The search tool is US-focused. Turkish listing snippets were obtained only via "extended" mode Turkish queries.

## Which international models are genuinely available in Türkiye, and at what TRY prices now?

### Takeaway
Thule (EnRoute, Subterra 2, Notus, Crossover 2), Samsonite (Securipak family), Wenger (Legacy 16"), Case Logic, Tomtoc, Targus, Peak Design (via Fujifilmshop), Fjällräven, Herschel and Eastpak all have Turkish online listings. Osprey has a Turkish presence (Hepsiburada store, ospreyturkiye.com.tr), but I found no confirmed Daylite Plus listing. Prices for the same model differ by 3-4x between sellers (for example Thule EnRoute 23L black, about 5.8k TL on Trendyol versus about 17k TL on Hepsiburada), so seller choice matters more than brand choice.

## Which best fit a 15-16" laptop plus chargers, notebook, one book, a few T-shirts and basic travel items (about 20-30 L)?

### Takeaway
Thule Subterra 2 21L (16" MacBook capacity, trolley strap) and Thule EnRoute 21L/23L fit the 20-30 L target and have the most Turkish listings in a sensible price range. Samsonite Securipak 2.0 (16 L) is probably too small for clothing plus laptop. Thule Subterra 2 27L (Akakce, from 8,052 TL) is the larger option.

### Cited Findings
- Subterra 2 21L is 46 x 32 x 20 cm, with a 25 x 37.5 cm laptop compartment that fits a 15.6" PC and a 16" MacBook. — [B&H](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/1841632-REG/thule_3205026_subterra_2_backpack_21l.html)
- Thule EnRoute is sold in 21L, 23L and 30L sizes in Türkiye, with 15.6" laptop designation on Akakce/Cimri titles. — [Akakce EnRoute 23L](https://www.akakce.com/laptop-cantasi/en-ucuz-thule-enroute-23l-ca-tebp316-15-6-notebook-sirt-cantasi-fiyati,370285212.html), [Vatan Thule](https://www.vatanbilgisayar.com/thule/notebook-cantalari/)
- Securipak 2.0 is 16 L. — [Samsonite UK](https://www.samsonite.co.uk/securipak-2.0-backpack-sage-15.6---light-sage/158828-2570.html)

### Inferences
- A 16" laptop plus a few T-shirts, underwear and a book will fit realistically in about 21-27 L. Securipak 2.0 at 16 L is likely too small for the clothing portion. Subterra 2 21L and EnRoute 23L are the best-evidenced matches; this is my judgement, not from a source.
