## Method and reliability caveats (read first)

- WebFetch failed on every Turkish domain I tried (DNS error: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND for donanimhaber.com, shiftdelete.net, webtekno.com, akakce.com, teknosa.com). A Bash call to the proxy status endpoint was denied by the permission classifier, so I did not work around it.
- All figures below therefore come from WebSearch result summaries of category and product pages. I could not open the pages, so ratings, review counts and prices are not verified against the live page. Treat them as "reported by search snapshot, unverified".
- Snapshot dates are unknown. Search ran on 2026-10-18, but each cached page's capture date is not shown. Prices are TRY and are labelled "snapshot, date unknown". Rankings change often.
- Review counts are often pooled across colour variants, and the same product can show different counts on different platforms.
- Several editorial pieces (Webtekno) are labelled by the search summary as brand collaborations (sponsored). Treat them as weaker evidence.
- Not covered: Trendyol and Hepsiburada "en çok satan" ordering (not observable via search), Ekşi Sözlük (one entry), Reddit r/Turkey (no Turkish thread found), YouTube, Şikayetvar, N11, Vatan and MediaMarkt ratings, ShiftDelete and Chip article contents.

## Key question 3: Recurring praise and complaints from Turkish buyers

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin because I could not open review sections. Praise centres on fit for 15.6" laptops and value for price, with one Lenovo B210 buyer reporting no leakage in heavy rain. Reported cautions are size mismatch (16"/16.5" laptops in a 15.6" bag), fabric bags needing a separate sleeve, and zippers being the weak point of "waterproof" claims. Counterfeit and durability complaints were not found.

### Cited Findings
- A buyer quoted in DonanımHaber says the Lenovo B210 did not leak in a downpour while riding a motorcycle and carried a 15.6" laptop comfortably — [DonanımHaber](https://www.donanimhaber.com/en-iyi-laptop-cantalari-onerileri--183701)
- Hepsiburada B210 reviews: interior volume is ample for laptop and accessories, with separate front and inner compartments. The 16" model is described as plain and good for daily use, with a warning that 16.5" laptops may not fit — [Hepsiburada B210 grey](https://www.hepsiburada.com/lenovo-15-6-inch-laptop-casual-backpack-b210-gri-4x40t84058-pm-HBC00005NTAPS)
- Ekşi Sözlük: one author praises Targus for versatile straps and says the laptop survived several drops, while cautioning that protection has limits — [Ekşi Sözlük](https://eksisozluk.com/entry/17490484)
- Technopat: one member advises against putting a laptop directly in a fabric bag and suggests a separate sleeve. Another notes 15.6" bags can fit 16.1" laptops — [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/laptop-icin-sirt-cantasi-onerisi.2368573/), [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/laptop-cantasi-onerisi.2822710/)
- Sellers' own wording shows zippers as the water weak point: an Idefix trekking pack is "waterproof except the zippers" — [Idefix](https://www.idefix.com/asker-kolisi-5510-litre-siyah-dagci-outdoor-kamp-trekking-sirt-cantasi-genisletilebilir-kamp-cantasi-p-9561965)
- Technopat, user asks about 18" laptop (ROG Strix G18): advice is to check dimensions and choose a 19"-compatible bag — [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/18-inc-laptop-icin-sirt-cantasi-onerisi.2906796/)
- A Trendyol "reviews" summary for B210 was generic and contained no concrete buyer complaints, so it is not used — [Trendyol](https://www.trendyol.com/lenovo/15-6-casual-laptop-sirt-cantasi-b210-acik-gri-p-289191437)

### Inferences
- "Su geçirmez" is marketing language. "Suya dayanıklı" fabric with unsealed zippers is the norm at budget prices, so only heavy-rain claims backed by buyer reports (as for B210) are meaningful.
- Size mismatch is the most predictable complaint category for 16"+ laptops (gaming, MacBook Pro 16").

### Gaps
- No quantified complaint frequency. No evidence found on counterfeit listings, zipper failures or comfort complaints for specific models. Şikayetvar, Trendyol review text and Reddit r/Turkey were not retrievable. Searching "fermuar koptu su geçirdi" returned only product pages.
