## Which are the best-sellers / best value with high review counts and good average ratings?

### Takeaway
The only high-volume, well-rated value candidate I could verify is the Trendyol D'Vers 15.6-16.9" "su geçirmez" laptop backpack (4.6 stars, about 6,800 ratings, though the search summary did not tie that figure to one exact listing). Decathlon's Commute 500 (23 L, TRY 1,650) and Quechua Escape 500 32 L (TRY 2,890) are the best-documented "real" travel-laptop options; Lenovo B210 is a well-reviewed budget commuter. Review counts for most other candidates were low or not visible.

## Which are really water-resistant/waterproof and have luggage pass-through?

### Takeaway
Only the Classone Savona BP-SV100 was explicitly described with both "su geçirmez" (WTX-Pro) fabric and a "bavul askı aparatı"; however the waterproof claim is a marketing term on polyester, not an IPX or seam-sealed rating. Most other "su geçirmez" laptop bags on Turkish marketplaces are only water-repellent, and none of my other candidates stated a trolley strap.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- "Su geçirmez" in Turkish listings should be treated as water-repellent unless seam-sealing, welded zippers or a rain cover are stated. A cheap dry-bag or rain cover is advisable for any of these.
- Luggage pass-through is under-labelled in Turkish listings; check photos for a vertical sleeve on the back panel, as sellers may not use the words "bavul geçiş".

## Known quality complaints (zipper, seams, strap wear, fake/low-quality sellers)?
